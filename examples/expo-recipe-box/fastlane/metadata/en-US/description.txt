Recipe Box keeps your favorite recipes and your grocery shopping in one simple, fast app.

BROWSE YOUR RECIPES
Flip through a clean list of recipes — each with cook time, ingredients, and step-by-step instructions. Tap any recipe to see the full method.

BUILD YOUR SHOPPING LIST
Check off what you already have and see what's left to buy. Your list is always a tap away.

SIMPLE BY DESIGN
No accounts, no clutter, no noise. Recipe Box opens straight to your recipes and gets out of your way.

This is a demo app shipped with listing-kit (https://github.com/bilal-/listing-kit) to show the kind of App Store and Google Play listing the tool generates directly from a repo.
