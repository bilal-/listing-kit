# Recipe Box privacy policy

_Last updated: October 4, 2026_

Recipe Box is the example app that ships with
[listing-kit](https://github.com/bilal-/listing-kit), an open-source tool for
generating App Store and Google Play listings. It exists to demonstrate that tool.

## What the app collects

Nothing. Recipe Box does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It
has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs that track
you.

## Network and storage

The app makes no network requests. Its recipes are bundled with the app, and the
shopping list and settings live only in memory while the app is open. Nothing is
written to your device's storage or sent anywhere, so closing the app discards them.

## Permissions

Recipe Box does not request access to your camera, location, contacts, photos, or
any other permission.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are made in this file, so its
[history](https://github.com/bilal-/listing-kit/commits/main/examples/expo-recipe-box/PRIVACY.md)
is the record of every change. Questions go to the
